Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to your most common questions.

When and where is DevConf 2026 taking place?

DevConf 2026 will be held from August 25-27, 2026, at the Grand Convention Center, featuring both main stage tracks and hands-on workshops.

Will the sessions be recorded for pass holders?

Yes, all keynotes and main track sessions are recorded. Access duration depends on your ticket type (30 days for Conference, lifetime for Pro/Team).

Are there team discounts available?

Absolutely! Our Team Pass offers a massive discount for up to 10 attendees, along with exclusive benefits like dedicated seating and recruitment perks.

Can I upgrade my ticket later?

Yes, you can upgrade from a Conference Pass to a Pro Pass at any time before August 20, 2026, by visiting your profile link, subject to availability.
